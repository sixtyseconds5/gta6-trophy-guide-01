    Roadmap to the Platinum

    55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. 28 are hidden on PlayStation until earned. None are online. Early estimates put the full list at roughly 200 hours: 60–80 hours of story, about 100 hours of checklist work, and about 10 hours of cleanup. Work the stages in order and the Platinum follows.

    • Keep cheats off. Two sources report that using cheats disables trophies and saving — the only fix mentioned is a clean save.
    • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. One source reports there is no mission replay, so a save is your only way back to a story moment.
    • Do the Chapter 1 meditation. Positive Mental Attitude is the one possibly missable trophy: Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 is reported to disappear when the chapter ends.
    • Start stranger chains as soon as they appear. One source reports their windows are tied to story progress.
